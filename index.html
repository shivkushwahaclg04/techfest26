FAQs

What is techFEST'26?

SLIET Longowal's annual technical fest with 14 domains, 50+ events and prizes worth over 5,00,000, built around the theme Technology and Sciences for Sustainable Earth.

Who can participate?

Students from colleges across India. Depending on the event, you can take part solo or in a team. Some events are for diploma students only.

How do I register?

Sign up for an account, complete your profile, then register for each event separately from the Register page. SLIET students are detected from their profile and pay nothing. You can also register through Unstop.

What are the fees?

Early bird: 299 per participant without accommodation, 599 with hostel accommodation (extra meals at 50 per diet). SLIET students register free. The fee is non-refundable.

Will I get a certificate?

Every registered participant gets a participation certificate, and winners take home prizes.
